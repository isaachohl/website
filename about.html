About — 02

Isaac Hohl is a versatile theatre performer trained in acting, dance, and voice — with the physical range to back it up.

A Niagara University BFA grad and trained baritone (A2–A4). On stage, he moves fluently between jazz, tap, ballet, and Irish contemporary dance. Off script, he's just as comfortable flying on trapeze, throwing a stage combat sequence, or clowning through physical theater. Add in tumbling, contact improv, and serious athletic conditioning, and you've got a performer built for anything a role demands.
